About the Republic

The Sincere Republic of Valerium is a sovereign digital state founded on the principles of transparent governance, civic responsibility, and digital innovation. Valerium operates as a fully functioning micronation within the digital sphere, providing its citizens with a framework for participation in governance, legislation, and community building.

Our motto — "Gens Valeria Dominum Non Recognoscit" — reflects our core belief that the people of Valerium recognize no master but the collective will of the citizenry.
