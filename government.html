Executive Authority

The Government

The Government of Valerium is the supreme executive authority of the State, entrusted with the administration of national systems, the preservation of order, and the enforcement of all enacted laws.

Structure

Departments & Offices

The administrative bodies responsible for executing the functions of the State.

Office of the President

The highest executive office, responsible for the overall direction and leadership of the Republic.

Ministry of Internal Affairs

Oversees domestic policy, citizenship administration, and internal security of the State.

Ministry of Foreign Affairs

Manages diplomatic relations, treaties, and international representation of Valerium.

Ministry of Justice

Ensures the fair application of law, manages the judicial system, and upholds constitutional rights.

Ministry of Innovation

Drives technological development, digital infrastructure, and the modernisation of state systems.

Ministry of Culture

Promotes national identity, cultural heritage, community engagement, and creative endeavours.
