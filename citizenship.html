Process

How to Become a Citizen

Follow these steps to apply for Valerian citizenship.

1️⃣

Join Discord

Connect with our community by joining the official Valerium Discord server, the primary hub for all national communications.

2️⃣

Submit Application

Complete the citizenship application form with the required details and submit it through the designated channel.

3️⃣

Get Approved

Once reviewed and approved by the Ministry of Internal Affairs, you will be granted full citizenship and access to the Republic.

Rights & Responsibilities

All citizens of Valerium are granted fundamental rights including the right to participate in governance, vote in elections, propose legislation, and hold public office. Citizens are expected to uphold the values of the Republic, respect fellow citizens, and contribute to the growth of the nation.
